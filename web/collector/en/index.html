Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Which e-commerce platforms does Xiaomie Magic Collector support?

It works on 1688, Taobao, Tmall, Amazon, AliExpress, Shopify, and virtually any independent store or blog/article page.

How does the Anti-Freeze Long-Image OCR work?

It runs a local Tesseract WASM SIMD engine inside your browser with chunked memory handling, extracting text from ultra-long supplier detail images without freezing your tab.

What is the 4-Level Carton & Spec Lookup Funnel?

It systematically checks structured DOM fields, spec tables, product descriptions, and OCR text from detail images to recover carton dimensions, weight, and 5 mandatory product specs.
