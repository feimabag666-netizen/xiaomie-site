Privacy Policy

Data Controller: XiaoMie Tools (Operated by Limei Chen)
Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · Privacy Contact: info@xiaomie.tools

1. Data We Collect

We practice strict data minimization and only collect information strictly necessary to authenticate your account and deliver paid entitlements:

  • Login Email Address: Used for passwordless OTP verification via noreply@xiaomie.tools and account lookup under api.xiaomie.tools.
  • Entitlement & Usage Ledger Data: Plan tier, activation timestamps, remaining credit counts, and deduction ledger entries scoped by (email, app_id).
  • Essential Billing Metadata: Transaction IDs, currency, plan identifier, and payment status provided by our Merchant of Record / payment processor to verify orders and issue instant access. Full payment card numbers are never stored on our servers.

2. Local-First Processing

Product long-image OCR, specification formatting, and WebCodecs video encoding run primarily locally inside your browser or desktop application. If you configure a personal third-party AI API Key (BYOK), requests go directly to your chosen provider under your own credentials.

3. No Sale of Personal Data

XiaoMie Tools does NOT sell, rent, lease, or trade user personal data or email addresses to third parties, data brokers, or advertisers.

4. Data Retention & Deletion Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or complete deletion of your account email and usage ledger at any time by emailing info@xiaomie.tools. Verified deletion requests are completed within 7 business days.
