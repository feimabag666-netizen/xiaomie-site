2. How to Request a Refund

  1. Send an email to info@xiaomie.tools with the subject line Refund Request.

  3. Our team will verify and approve eligible requests within 1–2 business days and issue the refund back to your original payment method via our Merchant of Record / payment processor. Depending on your card issuer or bank, funds typically appear within 5–10 business days.

Refunds apply directly to software fees, subscriptions, and licenses paid to XiaoMie Tools. Any third-party API usage charges incurred under a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) configuration are billed independently by your third-party API provider and are outside XiaoMie Tools' billing scope.
